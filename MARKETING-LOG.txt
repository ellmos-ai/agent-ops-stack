1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
agent-ops-stack provides the local-first operational coordination harness for multi-agent
CLI coding workflows (Claude Code, OpenAI Codex, Google Antigravity / Gemini, Moonshot
Kimi). It composes seven modular pillars:
  - dev-bricks/ticket-master (ticket-routing)
  - dev-bricks/lock-master (locking)
  - dev-bricks/sync-master (file-sync)
  - ellmos-ai/build-your-users-mind (decision-avatar)
  - ellmos-ai/skills (skill-pack)
  - ellmos-ai/ellmos-controlcenter-mcp (control-plane)
  - ellmos-ai/ellmos-homebase-mcp (mcp-runtime, memory/task facade)

This Pfad B upgrade elevates the repository to the ecosystem gold standard with a 14-point
quick navigation architecture, dual Mermaid architecture and lifecycle sequence diagrams,
10 formal governance invariants, a 12-repository sibling matrix, PEP 621 packaging,
multi-OS CI workflow, and an automated 11-test contract test suite.
